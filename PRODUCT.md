# Product

## Register

product

## Users

US rewards-card users who carry several cards and want a fast, trustworthy answer on a phone. They use CardRouter while making a purchase, planning a booking, activating a promotion, or checking whether a valuable card benefit is about to expire.

## Product Purpose

CardRouter helps people use the cards they already hold more effectively. It recommends the right card for a purchase and prevents valuable credits and benefits from going unused. Success means the user gets a clear next action in seconds, understands the value of that action, and never needs to share card numbers, issuer credentials, or financial-account data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quietly helpful. CardRouter should feel as immediate and trustworthy as Apple Wallet, with the considered organization and restraint of Things.

## Anti-references

- Crowded personal-finance dashboards that show every available metric at once
- Endless grids of visually identical cards
- Anxious urgency, artificial scarcity, and notification spam
- Childish gamification, streak pressure, and decorative celebration
- Generic fintech styling that substitutes polish for clarity

## Design Principles

1. **Show the next useful action.** Lead with what matters now, not everything the system knows.
2. **Reveal detail progressively.** Keep the primary path fast and make terms, history, and configuration available on demand.
3. **Earn every interruption.** Surface reminders only when they are timely, actionable, and likely to save meaningful value.
4. **Make value concrete.** Translate rewards and benefits into understandable outcomes without overstating certainty.
5. **Preserve local-first privacy.** Deliver meaningful personalization without requiring issuer logins, card numbers, or financial-account aggregation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support screen-reader semantics, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, color-independent status communication, and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. Use plain language and progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load.
