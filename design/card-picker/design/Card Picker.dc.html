Add a card
The + in the My cards header replaces the old full-width button, so the held list owns the whole screen. Tapping + pushes an Add a card page: search on top, browse tiles below, custom card at the bottom. The catalog is the {{ prodCount }} cards in the app today.
No autofocus. The search field sits at the top of the page unfocused; forcing the keyboard up would cover the list it sits above.
Custom card is a step in the same navigation stack (screen 6), not a modal stacked on the picker.
Card subtitles come from bestForCard(), derived from real earn rates, not a stored copy field.
Open question: three browse tiles are dead ends against today's catalog. Hotel cards has no cards, Airline cards has two, Other holds only Bilt, Apple and Target. Decide whether empty shelves hide or show a count.
